    Two connected experiences

    Use Mochi on the web, on your Mac, or both.

    Start in your browser today. Add the native Mac app when you want Mochi floating on your desktop.

    🌐

    Mochi Web Companion

    A browser-based experience. No download needed.

    • AI conversations and voice while the page is open
    • Live speech transcription
    • Memory Vault and Mood Center
    • Creative Studio and Vocal Personality Studio
    • Hydration tracking and focus reminders
    • Browser notifications when permitted
    🖥️

    Mochi Native Mac App

    A downloadable macOS app for a deeper desktop experience.

    • Floating, frameless, transparent mascot
    • Always-on-top and draggable
    • Edge popups from left, right, top, or bottom
    • Global keyboard shortcuts and quick voice activation
    • Native notifications and menu bar integration
    • CLI commands through Mac Terminal
    ⇄

    Synchronized Experience

    Choose Web Companion, the Mac app, or both. Signed-in users can sync preferences, approved memories, and supported account data between devices.

    Start on the Web

    System-wide floating features require the native Mac app. Browser features stay available on their own.

      Your little world stays yours.

      Privacy is part of how Mochi is built, not an afterthought. You decide what Mochi hears, remembers, and shows.

      • 🧠
        User-controlled memoryOnly approved memories are kept.
      • 🎙️
        Explicit microphone permissionMochi listens only when you turn it on.
      • ●
        Visible listening statusYou can always see when Mochi is listening.
      • 🚫
        No secret recordingNothing is recorded in the background.
      • 🔗
        Secure backend API communicationRequests go through the Mochi backend.

        Optional notifications & startupBoth are off until you choose them.
      • 🗑️
        Memory deletion controlsDelete any memory whenever you like.

        Data export & transparent permissionsTake your data with you and review every permission.
